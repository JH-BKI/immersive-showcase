Enhancing the way we learn

What is immersive learning?

Immersive learning surrounds the learner with a realistic environment they can move through and act in. Instead of reading about a procedure, the learner can now carry it out, explore the scenario and safely make mistakes and see what happens.

It works best for skills that are physical, risky, expensive to practise, or tied to a specific place.

01 · 3D content

  1. 3D content

    Every immersive experience starts with 3D assets. We model equipment, environments and characters from scratch, or scan real objects and places with photogrammetry, so learners practise on exactly what they'll use.

    • Modelled and scanned assets
    • Optimised for headsets and the web
    • Reusable across courses and updates

  2. 360° scenes and video

    Real locations captured with 360° cameras and layered with hotspots, questions and branching paths. Quick to produce, easy to update, and it works on any device.

    • Hotspots, quizzes and branching
    • Runs on desktop, phone or headset
    • Ideal for orientation and site familiarisation

  3. Extended reality (XR)

    Virtual and mixed reality on headsets like Meta Quest and Apple Vision Pro, from fully simulated worlds to 3D content blended into the room around you.

    • Hands-on procedures and high-risk tasks
    • Seated and standing comfort modes
    • Headset fleet setup and support

The process

From learning outcome to immersive content

The same four stages behind every eWorks immersive project.

1 · Discover
  1. 1

    Discover

    During planning workshops, our team maps the content, outcomes, learner needs alongside teaching staff to plan the path forward.

  2. 2

    Design

    Our designers take the requirements and produce storyboards, spatial flow diagrams and scripts, reviewed with our subject matter experts.

  3. 3

    Develop

    We create the required experience, including images, video, animations, scripts and 3D assets, all using our in-house digital media talent at eWorks.

  4. 4

    Deploy

    Published to the LMS for use directly inside the course. Tracking via SCORM/xAPI can be enabled.
